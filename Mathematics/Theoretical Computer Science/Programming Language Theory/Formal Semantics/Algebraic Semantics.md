*Algebraic Semantics* is a kind of [[Formal Semantics]] that represents [[Program|Programs]] as [[Algebraic Structure|Algebraic Structures]].
* A form of [[Axiomatic Semantics]], but its algebraic laws can also be interpreted [[Denotational Semantics|denotationally]] and [[Operational Semantics|operationally]]
# Examples
  * In [[Digital Signal Processing|DSP]], the [[Transfer Function]] of a [[System]] can be represented in [[Computer Science/Computational Science/Digital Signal Processing/Operator|Operator Notation]], which is an [[Point-Free]], algebraic form of a [[Difference Equation]]
