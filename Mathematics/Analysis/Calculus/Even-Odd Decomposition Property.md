Any [[Mathematics/SetTheory/Function|Function]] can be *uniquely* decomposed into the sum of an [[Even Function]] and an [[Odd Function]]
$$
\begin{align}
x(t) &= \mathbb{O}(t) + \mathbb{E}(t)\\
&= \frac{x(t) + x(-t)}{2} + \frac{x(t) - x(-t)}{2}\\
&= \frac{x(t) + x(-t) + x(t) - x(-t)}{2}\\
&= \frac{2x(t)}{2}\\
&= x(t)\\
\end{align}
$$
