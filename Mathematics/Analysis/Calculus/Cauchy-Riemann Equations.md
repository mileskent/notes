The *Cauchy-Riemann Equations* are a system of [[Partial Differential Equation|Partial Differential Equations]] whose satisfaction exactly corresponds with the [[Differentiable|Differentiability]] of its [[Complex Number|Complex]] [[Computer Science/Architecture/Programming Languages/Function]] inputs.
$$
\begin{align}
u_{x} &= v_{y}\\
u_{y} &= -v_{x}\\
\end{align}
$$
