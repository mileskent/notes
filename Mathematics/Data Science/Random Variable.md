A *Random Variable* is a [[Mathematics/Set Theory/Function|Function]] $X$ that maps each [[Outcome]] $\omega$ of the [[Sample Space]] $S$ of a [[Random Experiment]] to a [[Real Number]] $x \in \mathbb{R}$

$$
X: \omega \in S \rightarrow x \in \mathbb{R}
$$

* the Random Variable is always denoted with a capital letter, and the real number is the corresponding lowercase letter, e.g. $X$ and $x$, $Y$ and $y$, etc.
* a Random Variable can be discrete or continuous
