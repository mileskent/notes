Covariance measures the direction of the linear relationship between two random variables.

$$
\text{Cov}(X,Y) \equiv
\sigma_{XY} \equiv
E\left[(X - E[X])(Y - E[Y])\right]
E[XY] - E[X]E[Y]
$$

- $\text{Cov}(X,X) = \text{Var}(X)$
- $\text{Cov}(X,Y) > 0 \implies$ Variables move in the same direction.
- $\text{Cov}(X,Y) < 0 \implies$ Variables move in opposite directions.
- $X$ and $Y$ are independent $\implies \text{Cov}(X,Y) = 0$
