# Disjoint Events
A special case of [[Principle of Inclusion-Exclusion for Probability]], when all events are [[Disjoint]]. This is because all terms after the first term are the [[Empty Set]] $\varnothing$
$$
P\left( \bigcup^{\infty}_{i=1} A_{i} \right) = \sum^{\infty}_{i=1}P(A_{i})
$$
