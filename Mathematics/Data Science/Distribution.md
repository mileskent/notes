* Distribution
	* [[Discrete Distribution]], A Distribution with a Discrete [[Random Variable]]
		* [[Discrete Uniform Distribution]]
		* [[Discrete Bernoulli Distribution]], for the probability of a single successful [[Bernoulli Trial]]
		* [[Discrete Binomial  Distribution]], like Bern but repeated; probability of x successes in n trials
		* [[Discrete Geometric  Distribution]], like Bern but probability of x trials being the first success
		* [[Discrete Negative Binomial  Distribution]], like Geo, but the rth success rather than the first
		* [[Discrete Poisson  Distribution]], probability of x [[Poisson Event]]s occuring in a fixed interval of time, for which $\lambda$ is the average rate of occurences per interval
	* [[Continuous Distribution]]
		* [[Continuous Uniform Distribution]]
		* [[Continuous Exponential Distribution]], probability density of the next [[Poisson Event]] being within x amount of domain time, given an average rate of occurence of $\lambda$, [[Cumulative Density Function|CDF]] for probability
		* [[Continuous Normal Distribution]], probability density of normal distribution, [[Cumulative Density Function|CDF]] for probability
			* [[Continuous Standard Normal Distribution]], Norm, but standardized.
