A *Partition* of a [[Sample Space]] is a [[Set]] of [[Event]]s that are [[Disjoint]] that form the [[Universe]]

$$
\text{Partition}(\{A_{1}, \dots, A_{n}\}) \iff 
\forall i, j \in [1, n] \text{ s.t. } i \neq j, A_{i} \cap A_{j} = \varnothing \land
S = \bigcup_{i=1}^n A_{i}
$$
