The [[Set]] of all possible outcomes of an [[Experiment]], usually denoted $S$ or $\Omega$. It is the [[Universe]] for [[Probability Theory]] where every [[Event]] $A$ satisfies $A \subseteq S$ (see [[Probability Theory#Correspondance to Set Theory]]).

### Finite Sample Space
A [[Sample Space]] where $|S| < \infty$ 

Means we can use [[Cardinality]] and [[Combinatorics]] with $P(A)=\frac{|A|}{|S|}$ to get [[Probability]]

#### Simple Sample Space
A [[#Finite Sample Space]] where all singleton outcomes are equiprobable: $P(\{\omega\})=1/|S|$ for every $\omega \in S$, so $P(A)=|A|/|S|$ for any [[Event]] $A \subseteq S$
