The Inner Product is a [[Bilinear Map|Bilinear]] map $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle : V \times V \to F$ satisfying:

- Symmetry: $\langle \vec{a}, \vec{b} \rangle = \langle \vec{b}, \vec{a} \rangle$
- Linearity: $\langle \alpha \vec{a} + \beta \vec{b}, \vec{c} \rangle = \alpha \langle \vec{a}, \vec{c} \rangle + \beta \langle \vec{b}, \vec{c} \rangle$
- Positive definiteness: $\langle \vec{a}, \vec{a} \rangle \geq 0$, with equality iff $\vec{a} = 0$

The standard inner product on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is the [[Dot Product]]:

$$\langle \vec{a}, \vec{b} \rangle = \sum_i a_i b_i = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$$
