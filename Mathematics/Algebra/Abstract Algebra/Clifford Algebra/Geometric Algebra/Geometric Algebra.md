Geometric Algebra is literally [[Linear Algebra]] BUT we can also multiply and divide by vectors
* This new property is called the [[Geometric Product]]
* A [[Vector]] that has the Geometric Product defined for it is called a [[Multivector]]
* If Linear Algebra is "the study of [[Vector Space|Vector Spaces]]", then Geometric Algebra is the study of [[Multivector Space]]s, otherwise known as [[Multivector Space|Geometric Algebra]]s. Such as space is considered an "Algebra" as in an [[Algebraic Structure]], because it is a space plus this geometric product. Specifically, it is considered an [[Algebra over a Field]]
* Geometric Algebra's claim to fame is that the same kind of transformation is expressed the same regardless of dimension, whereas in Linear Algebra, the transformation matrix has to scale with its operand.

# Applications

- Projective Geometric Algebra (PGA): 3D geometry with unified rotations, translations, reflections, and intersections
- Conformal Geometric Algebra (CGA): extends PGA to handle circles, spheres, and inversions
- Spacetime Algebra (STA): $\mathbb{G}(1,3)$, used in special relativity and electromagnetism (unifies Maxwell's four equations into one)
- Computer graphics and robotics: rotors replace rotation matrices and quaternions
- Inverse kinematics: sandwich product uniformly handles translation along and rotation about a line
- Physics: spinors, quantum mechanics
