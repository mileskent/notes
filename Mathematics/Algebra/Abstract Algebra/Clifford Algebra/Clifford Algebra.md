A *Clifford Algebra* $\mathbb{G}(p, q, r)$ is an Associative Algebra built from a [[Vector Space]] with a [[Quadratic Form]], defined by the contraction axiom:

$$\vec{v}^2 = Q(\vec{v})$$

for all vectors $\vec{v}$, where $Q$ is the quadratic form. The signature $(p, q, r)$ specifies how many basis vectors square to $+1$, $-1$, and $0$ respectively.

[[Geometric Algebra]] is the special case over $\mathbb{R}$ with the Euclidean quadratic form $Q(\vec{v}) = |\vec{v}|^2$, so all basis vectors square to $+1$: $\mathbb{G}^n = \mathbb{G}(n, 0, 0)$.

# Notable Clifford Algebras

| Algebra | Signature | Description |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| $\mathbb{G}^3$ | $(3,0,0)$ | Standard 3D Geometric Algebra |
| $\mathbb{G}(3,0,1)$ | $(3,0,1)$ | Projective Geometric Algebra (PGA) |
| $\mathbb{G}(4,1,0)$ | $(4,1,0)$ | Conformal Geometric Algebra (CGA) |
| $\mathbb{G}(1,3,0)$ | $(1,3,0)$ | Spacetime Algebra (STA) |
| $\mathbb{G}(0,2,0)$ | $(0,2,0)$ | Quaternion algebra ($i^2=j^2=k^2=-1$) |
