In [[Optics]], the Beer-Lambert Law describes the [[Physics/Electromagnetism/Attenuation|Attenuation]] of [[Light]] as it propogates, as an equation.
$$
I(z) = I_{0} e^{-\alpha z}
$$
$\alpha$ : [[Absorption Coefficient]]
$z$ : Path length through the medium

* [[Physics/Electromagnetism/Attenuation]] = $\large \frac{I(z)}{I_0} = e^{-\alpha z}$
* $-\log$ [[Physics/Electromagnetism/Attenuation]] $= \alpha z \equiv$ [[Absorbance]] $\equiv$ [[Optical Density]]
