A Cascaded System is a composition of [[System|systems]] where the output of one system is the input of the next.

# Categorical Structure

In [[Category Theory]], cascading is exactly [[Composition]] of [[Morphism|morphisms]]. If $H_1$ and $H_2$ are LTI systems, the cascade $H_2 \circ H_1$ is their composed morphism. Composition is associative, so $(H_3 \circ H_2) \circ H_1 = H_3 \circ (H_2 \circ H_1)$.
