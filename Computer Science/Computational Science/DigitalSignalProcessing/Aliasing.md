[[Computer Science/Computational Science/DigitalSignalProcessing/Signal|Signals]] that are [[Sampling|sampled]] with too low of a [[Sampling#Sampling Frequency]] can appear as lower frequencies; using an insufficiently high sampling frequency can cause totally different [[Analog Signal|Continuous-Time Signals]] to look identical in their [[Discrete-Time Signal|Discrete-Time]] forms. The minimum sampling frequency needed to reconstruct a given CT signal is given by the [[Nyquist-Shannon Sampling Theorem]].
