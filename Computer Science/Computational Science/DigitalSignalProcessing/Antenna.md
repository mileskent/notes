An *antenna* is a transducer that converts between [[Computer Science/Computational Science/DigitalSignalProcessing/Signal|electrical signals]] and [[Electromagnetic Wave|electromagnetic waves]]. 
* In transmit mode it radiates RF energy into free space
* In receive mode it captures incoming RF energy and delivers it as an electrical signal to the [[Receiver]].
* An antenna is a passive component, it only converts signals. This is different from a [[Radar]] which is a complete active system that uses an antenna (or multiple antennas) as one part alongside a [[Transmitter]], [[Receiver]], and signal processor to detect and locate objects.
