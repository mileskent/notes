A *receiver* is a device that 
1. Captures an incoming signal using an [[Antenna]]
2. Where that signal is [[Windowing|Windowed]] around a particular center frequency that is "tuned" (determined) by a Tuner, i.e. the [[Instantaneous Bandwidth]]
3. Where an Amplifier boosts the amplitude of the signal to something useable
4. Where a [[Filter]] removes out-of-band interference before digitization
5. Where an [[Analog-to-Digital Converter|ADC]] digitizes the analog baseband signal into [[IQ data]]]
