An *Operator* is an [[Mathematical Object|Object]] that manipulates [[Computer Science/Computational Science/DigitalSignalProcessing/Signal|Signals]]. It is a [[fold]] for signals. Operators can have arbitrarily many signal inputs, but unary operators (e.g. inverter) and binary operators (e.g. add) are most common.
