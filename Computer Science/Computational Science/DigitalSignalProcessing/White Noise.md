*White noise* is a random signal with equal [[Power Spectrum|power]] at every frequency, a flat power spectral density. The name is by analogy with white light, which contains all visible frequencies equally.

$$
S(f) = N_0
$$

Because all frequencies are equally represented, white noise samples are uncorrelated: knowing one sample tells you nothing about any other.

White noise is an idealization, integrating a flat $S(f)$ over all frequencies gives infinite total power. In practice, noise is always band-limited.
