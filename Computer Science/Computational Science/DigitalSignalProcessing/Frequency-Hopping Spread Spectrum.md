*Frequency-Hopping Spread Spectrum* (FHSS) is a [[Spread Spectrum]] technique in which the carrier frequency is rapidly switched among many frequencies according to a [[Pseudo-Noise|PN sequence]] known to both transmitter and receiver. This spreads the signal's energy across a wide [[Bandwidth]] over time, providing resistance to narrowband interference and jamming.
