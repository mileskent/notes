A *symbol* is one discrete transmitted value — e.g. one [[Chip]], one BPSK bit, or one [[Quadrature Amplitude Modulation|QAM]] point. The number of bits per symbol is $\log_2 M$, where $M$ is the [[Constellation Order]]. The symbol rate is related to the [[Computer Science/Networking/Bandwidth|data rate]] by:

$$
R_{data} = R_{symbol} \cdot \log_2 M
$$
