A Difference Equation is a [[Differential Equation]], but for [[Discrete-Time Signal|Discrete-Time]] [[System|Systems]]. Anywhere you'd see a [[Derivative]] in a differential equation, you'd see a difference of discrete [[Computer Science/Computational Science/Digital Signal Processing/Signal|Signals]]. It is easy to see relationships between systems using [[Linear Unary Operator#Operator Notation]], which is facilitated by the algebraic form of a system.
