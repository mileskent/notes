Radio Frequency bands are standardized ranges of the Electromagnetic Spectrum used for wireless communication, [[Radar]], and sensing. 

# ITU Band Classification
This classification system covers all possible frequencies.

| Band | Name                        | Frequency       | Wavelength        | Common Uses                  |
| ---- | --------------------------- | --------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------- |
| ELF  | Extremely Low Frequency     | 3-30 Hz         | 100,000-10,000 km | Submarine comms              |
| SLF  | Super Low Frequency         | 30-300 Hz       | 10,000-1,000 km   | Submarine comms              |
| ULF  | Ultra Low Frequency         | 300 Hz - 3 kHz  | 1,000-100 km      | Underground comms            |
| VLF  | Very Low Frequency          | 3-30 kHz        | 100-10 km         | Navigation, time signals     |
| LF   | Low Frequency               | 30-300 kHz      | 10-1 km           | AM longwave, navigation      |
| MF   | Medium Frequency            | 300 kHz - 3 MHz | 1 km - 100 m      | AM broadcast radio           |
| HF   | High Frequency              | 3-30 MHz        | 100-10 m          | Shortwave, military, amateur |
| VHF  | Very High Frequency         | 30-300 MHz      | 10-1 m            | FM radio, TV, ATC, marine    |
| UHF  | Ultra High Frequency        | 300 MHz - 3 GHz | 1 m - 10 cm       | TV, cell, GPS, ADS-B, WiFi   |
| SHF  | Super High Frequency        | 3-30 GHz        | 10-1 cm           | Radar, satellite, 5G         |
| EHF  | Extremely High Frequency    | 30-300 GHz      | 10-1 mm           | Millimeter wave, 5G, imaging |
| THF  | Tremendously High Frequency | 300 GHz - 3 THz | 1-0.1 mm          | Terahertz imaging, research  |

# IEEE Band Classification
Used in radar, satellite, and defense contexts. These overlay on the ITU bands, and only cover a subset of the spectrum.

| Band | Frequency       | Wavelength  |
| ---- | --------------- | ----------- |
| HF   | 3-30 MHz        | 10-100 m    |
| VHF  | 30-300 MHz      | 1-10 m      |
| UHF  | 300 MHz - 1 GHz | 30-100 cm   |
| L    | 1-2 GHz         | 15-30 cm    |
| S    | 2-4 GHz         | 7.5-15 cm   |
| C    | 4-8 GHz         | 3.75-7.5 cm |
| X    | 8-12 GHz        | 2.5-3.75 cm |
| Ku   | 12-18 GHz       | 1.67-2.5 cm |
| K    | 18-27 GHz       | 1.1-1.67 cm |
| Ka   | 27-40 GHz       | 0.75-1.1 cm |
| V    | 40-75 GHz       | 4-7.5 mm    |
| W    | 75-110 GHz      | 2.7-4 mm    |
| mm   | 110-300 GHz     | 1-2.7 mm    |
