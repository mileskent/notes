*Baseband* refers to the original [[Frequency]] range ([[Band]]) of a [[Computer Science/Computational Science/Digital Signal Processing/Signal|Signal]] before it is [[Modulation|modulated]] onto a [[Carrier Wave]]. In a [[Receiver]], the incoming RF signal is mixed down to baseband for processing, centering the signal of interest around 0 Hz. In digital systems, baseband signals are represented as [[IQ data]].
