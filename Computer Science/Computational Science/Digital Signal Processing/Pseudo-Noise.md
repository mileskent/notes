A *pseudo-noise* (PN) sequence is a deterministic binary sequence that is designed to appear statistically random. It has a flat [[Power Spectrum]] and low [[Cross-Correlation|autocorrelation]] at any non-zero lag, like [[Noise|white noise]]. But unlike true noise it is known and reproducible, so the [[Receiver]] can generate the identical sequence and use it to despread the signal via [[Cross-Correlation]].
