*Instantaneous bandwidth* is the total [[Bandwidth|bandwidth]] $B$ of [[Frequency Spectrum]] captured by a [[Receiver]] in a single snapshot. It defines a hard ceiling on how much spectrum is simultaneously observable. Any signal outside this [[Windowing|Window]] is missed unless the receiver retunes.

$$
B_{inst} = f_s
$$

where $f_{s}$ is the [[Sampling#Sampling Frequency]] of the [[Analog-to-Digital Converter|ADC]]
