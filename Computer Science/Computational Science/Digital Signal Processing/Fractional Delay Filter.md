A *fractional delay filter* is a [[Filter]] that delays a [[Discrete-Time Signal]] by a non-integer phase shift $\mu$. 
* Since discrete-time systems can only shift by integer samples, fractional delay must be approximated by interpolation between samples. Common implementations include [[Lagrange Polynomial Interpolation]] and the [[Farrow Interpolation]].
