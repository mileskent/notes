The convolution theorem states that the [[Fourier Transform]] of a [[Convolution]] of two functions is equal to the product of their Fourier transforms.

$$
\mathcal{F}\{x[n] * h[n]\} = X(e^{jw}) \cdot H(e^{jw})
$$

# Categorical Structure

From [[Category Theory]] perspective, the [[Fourier Transform]] is an [[Isomorphism]] from the space of signals under [[Convolution]] to the space of spectra under pointwise multiplication: it converts the convolution product to ordinary multiplication, and the inverse Fourier transform is its inverse morphism.
