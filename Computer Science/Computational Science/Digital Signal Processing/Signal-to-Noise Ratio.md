*Signal-to-Noise Ratio* (SNR) is the ratio of  [[Signal Power]] to [[Noise]] power, typically expressed in decibels.
$$
\text{SNR}_{dB} = 10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{P_{signal}}{P_{noise}}\right)
$$

* where higher SNR means the signal is stronger relative to the noise floor, making detection and demodulation more reliable
* where the noise power scales with [[Bandwidth]]: $P_{noise} = N_{0} \cdot B$
