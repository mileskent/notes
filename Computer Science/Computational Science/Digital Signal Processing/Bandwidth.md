*Bandwidth* is the width of a [[Band]], the difference between the upper and lower frequency limits of a signal or channel. You can also think of it as the spread of a [[Computer Science/Computational Science/Digital Signal Processing/Signal|Signal]]'s [[Frequency Spectrum]] in the frequency domain.

$$
B = f_{high} - f_{low}
$$
