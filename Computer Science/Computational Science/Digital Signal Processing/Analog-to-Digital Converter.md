An *Analog-to-Digital Converter* (ADC) converts a continuous [[Analog Signal]] into a [[Discrete-Time Signal]] by performing two operations:
1. **Sampling**: measures the signal amplitude at discrete time intervals determined by the sample rate $f_s$.
2. **Quantization**: rounds each measured amplitude to the nearest value in a finite set of levels, introducing [[Quantization|quantization error]].
