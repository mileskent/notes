*VITA-49* is a standard published by the VMEbus International Trade Association that defines the [[VRT|VITA Radio Transport (VRT)]] protocol, a packet-based format for transporting digitized [[IQ data]] and associated metadata over a network. A VITA-49 stream consists of two packet types:

* Data packets: contain [[IQ data|IQ]] samples.
* Context packets: carry metadata such as center frequency, sample rate, gain, and timestamps.

VITA-49 is widely used in software-defined radio systems to decouple RF hardware from signal processing software.
