A [[Analog Signal|Continuous-Time Signal]] can be reconstructed from its [[Sampling|samples]] if and only if the [[Sampling#Sampling Frequency]] is at least twice its highest frequency component.

$$
f_{s} > 2 f_{\text{max}}
$$
* $2f_{\text{max}}$ is called the *Nyquist Rate*
* Violation of this condition causes distortion called [[Aliasing]]
